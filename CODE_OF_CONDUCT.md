# Code of Conduct

BotParty should be a welcoming place for contributors and users.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.
- Accept feedback in good faith and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep project spaces free of harassment, discrimination, threats, and sexualized attention.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces run by the BotParty project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to `security@botparty.live`. Reports are handled privately. Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, and history of an incident. Anyone involved in a report must avoid conflicts of interest and protect the privacy of the people involved.
